Після модальних дієслів і `seem` (а також після `to`) потрібна **правильна форма інфінітива**. Вона залежить від того, **який час** мав би дієслово у звичайному реченні.

## Чотири форми

| Час у прямому реченні | Форма інфінітива | Як будується |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present Simple, Future Simple | **Present Infinitive** | (to) Verb |
| Present Continuous, Future Continuous | **Continuous Infinitive** | (to) be Verb+**ing** |
| Present Perfect, Past Simple, Past Perfect, Future Perfect | **Perfect Infinitive** | (to) have Verb+**ed** / 3rd form |
| Present Perfect Continuous, Past Continuous, Past Perfect Continuous, Future Perfect Continuous | **Perfect Continuous Infinitive** | (to) have been Verb+**ing** |

## Приклади

| Форма | Приклади |
| --- | --- |
| **Present Infinitive** — (to) work | She wanted **to work** at home. <br> She should **work** at home every day. |
| **Continuous Infinitive** — (to) be working | She seems **to be working** at home today. <br> She should **be working** at home now. |
| **Perfect Infinitive** — (to) have worked / written | She seems **to have written** a book last year. <br> She should **have written** a book last year. |
| **Perfect Continuous Infinitive** — (to) have been working | She seems **to have been writing** a book at two pm yesterday. <br> She should **have been writing** a book at two pm yesterday. |

## Як обрати форму

Уявіть звичайне речення і його час:

1. *She **works** at home every day.* / *She will work at home.* → Present Infinitive: `work`
2. *She **is working** at home now.* → Continuous Infinitive: `be working`
3. *She **wrote** a book last year.* / *She has written…* → Perfect Infinitive: `have written`
4. *She **was writing** a book at two pm yesterday.* → Perfect Continuous Infinitive: `have been writing`

Після **модальних дієслів** (`may`, `should`, `must`…) `to` не потрібне, після **seem** — потрібне. Див. також: «Модальні дієслова: припущення», «Seem — здаватися».
