Непряма мова передає слова іншої людини **без цитування**. Зазвичай час у підрядній частині **зсувається на крок назад** (backshift).

## Зсув часів

| Пряма мова | Непряма мова |
| --- | --- |
| Present Simple: "I **work**." | Past Simple: She said she **worked**. |
| Present Continuous: "I **am working**." | Past Continuous: She said she **was working**. |
| Present Perfect: "I **have finished**." | Past Perfect: She said she **had finished**. |
| Past Simple: "I **finished**." | Past Perfect: She said she **had finished**. |
| will: "I **will** call." | would: She said she **would** call. |
| can: "I **can** swim." | could: She said she **could** swim. |
| may: "I **may** come." | might: She said she **might** come. |
| must: "I **must** go." | had to: She said she **had to** go. |

**Не змінюються:** `would`, `could`, `should`, `might`, `Past Perfect`. Якщо повідомлення **досі актуальне** або головне дієслово в теперішньому (*She says…*), зсуву не буде.

## Слова часу та місця

| Пряма | Непряма |
| --- | --- |
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| yesterday | the day before |
| here | there |
| this / these | that / those |
| ago | before |

## Say і tell

- **say** (без особи): *She **said** (that) she was tired.*
- **tell** (з особою): *She **told me** (that) she was tired.*

## Запитання

Порядок слів як у **розповідному** реченні, без допоміжних `do/does/did`.

- "Where **do you live**?" → He asked me **where I lived**.
- Питання так/ні → **if / whether**: "Are you ready?" → She asked **if I was ready**.

## Накази та прохання

`told / asked + особа + to-infinitive`:

- "Close the door." → She **told me to close** the door.
- "Don't be late." → She **told me not to be** late.

## Типові помилки

- ✗ *She said me that she was tired.* → ✓ *She **told** me / She **said** (that)…*
- ✗ *He asked where did I live.* → ✓ *He asked where **I lived**.*
- ✗ *She told me don't be late.* → ✓ *She told me **not to be** late.*
- ✗ *He said he will call tomorrow.* (повідомлення вже неактуальне) → ✓ *He said he **would** call the next day.*
