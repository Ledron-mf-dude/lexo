**Time markers** — слова, які підказують, який час обрати. Вони не діють механічно (контекст важливіший), але в більшості речень допомагають не помилитися.

## Шпаргалка

| Час | Типові маркери |
| --- | --- |
| **Present Simple** | always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, every day, on Mondays, twice a week |
| **Present Continuous** | now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, currently, these days, Look! Listen! |
| **Past Simple** | yesterday, last week / year, ago, in 2019, when I was a child, then |
| **Past Continuous** | at 8 pm yesterday, at that moment, while, all day yesterday |
| **Present Perfect** | ever, never, just, already, yet, so far, recently, lately, this year, for, since |
| **Past Perfect** | before, after, by the time, already (у розповіді про минуле) |
| **Future** | tomorrow, next week, soon, in two days, tonight, one day, I think, probably |

## Present Simple: як часто

- *She **usually** takes the bus.* / *I go to the gym **every day**.* / *He calls me **twice a week**.*
- Прислівники частоти стоять **перед** змістовим дієсловом, але **після** `be`: *She **always** arrives on time.* / *She **is always** on time.*

## Present Continuous: зараз, тимчасово

- *I'm working **at the moment**.* / *She's living in Warsaw **these days**.* / ***Look!** It's snowing.*
- `always` з Continuous — роздратування: ***He's always losing** his keys!*

## Past Simple чи Present Perfect?

| Past Simple — час **названо** | Present Perfect — час **не названо** або період триває |
| --- | --- |
| I saw him **yesterday**. | I've **just** seen him. |
| She left **two hours ago**. | She has **already** left. |
| We met **in 2020**. | We've known each other **since** 2020. |
| I was ill **last week**. | I've been ill **this week**. (тиждень ще триває) |

## Future: скоро і потім

- *I'll call you **tomorrow**.* / *We're flying to Rome **next week**.* / *The train leaves **in ten minutes**.*

## Маркери, що бувають у кількох часах

| Маркер | Приклади |
| --- | --- |
| **today / this week** | Present Continuous (зараз), Present Perfect (результат), Past Simple (закінчена частина) |
| **for** | *I've lived here **for** years* (досі) — *I lived there **for** years* (уже ні) |
| **when** | *When **did** you arrive?* — Past Simple (не Present Perfect) |

## Маркери Perfect Continuous і Future Perfect

| Час | Маркери |
| --- | --- |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | for, since, how long, all day, lately, recently |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | for … when, how long … before |
| **Future Continuous** | this time tomorrow, at 8 tomorrow |
| **Future Perfect** | by, by then, by the time, by the end of |

## Маркер — не закон

Той самий маркер буває в різних часах, рішення залежить від змісту:

- ***Just*** — Present Perfect у BrE, Past Simple в AmE: *I've just eaten / I just ate.*
- ***Always*** — звичка (*He always helps*) або роздратування з Continuous (*He's always complaining*).
- ***Now*** — дія зараз (*I'm working now*) або стан (*I live in Kyiv now*).

## Типові помилки

- ✗ *I have seen him yesterday.* → ✓ *I **saw** him yesterday.*
- ✗ *I am knowing him since 2010.* → ✓ *I **have known** him since 2010.*
- ✗ *Look! She works in the garden now.* (дія саме зараз) → ✓ *Look! She **is working** in the garden now.*
- ✗ *When have you arrived?* → ✓ *When **did** you arrive?*
- ✗ *This time tomorrow I will fly to Rome.* → ✓ *This time tomorrow I **will be flying** to Rome.*
- ✗ *By the end of the month I will finished.* → ✓ *By the end of the month I **will have finished**.*
