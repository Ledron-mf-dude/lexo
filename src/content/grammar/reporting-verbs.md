Замість нейтрального `said` у непрямій мові часто вживають **точніше дієслово**, яке передає намір. Схема після дієслова залежить від нього.

## Схеми

| Схема | Дієслова | Приклад |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **+ that-clause** | say, explain, admit, claim, complain, mention, suggest, insist | She **admitted that** she was wrong. |
| **+ to-infinitive** | refuse, promise, offer, agree, threaten, demand | He **refused to help**. |
| **+ особа + to-infinitive** | advise, ask, tell, warn, remind, invite, encourage, order, persuade | She **advised me to rest**. |
| **+ -ing** | admit, deny, suggest, recommend, mention | He **denied taking** the money. |
| **+ прийменник + -ing** | apologise for, insist on, accuse sb of, blame sb for, thank sb for, congratulate sb on | She **apologised for being** late. |

## Приклади: пряма → непряма

| Пряма мова | Reported |
| --- | --- |
| "I'll pay." | He **offered to pay**. / He **promised to pay**. |
| "I didn't take it." | He **denied taking** it. |
| "Don't go alone." | She **warned me not to go** alone. |
| "Why don't we go out?" | He **suggested going** out. / He **suggested that we go** out. |
| "Sorry I'm late." | She **apologised for being late**. |
| "You stole the money!" | He **accused her of stealing** the money. |
| "Well done!" | They **congratulated me on winning**. |

## Suggest: три способи

- *She suggested **going** to the cinema.*
- *She suggested **(that) we go** / **we should go** to the cinema.*
- Не **suggest me to go**: *She suggested me to go* — помилка.

Після `suggest`, `insist`, `recommend` у формальному стилі вживають subjunctive: *She suggested that he **see** a doctor.* Див. «Subjunctive: suggest that he go, it's essential that».

## Заперечення

`not` ставимо перед інфінітивом: *He **told me not to worry**.* / *She **promised not to tell**.*

## Ще дієслова

| Дієслово | Схема | Приклад |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **beg** | + особа + to | She **begged** him **to stay**. |
| **urge** | + особа + to | They **urged** us **to leave**. |
| **object to** | + -ing | He **objected to paying**. |
| **boast about / of** | + -ing | She **boasted about winning**. |
| **explain** | + (to особа) + that / wh- | He **explained** (to me) **why** he was late. |
| **announce** | + that | They **announced that** the shop would close. |
| **claim** | + to / that | He **claims to know** her. |
| **forbid** | + особа + to | They **forbade** us **to enter**. |

## Звичайна мова чи reporting verb?

У розмові частіше просто *said / told / asked*; точні дієслова (*admitted, denied, insisted*) — у письмових переказах, новинах, на іспитах.

## Як вибрати

1. Після дієслова — *to* + дієслово: *agree, offer, promise, refuse, threaten, claim*.
2. Після дієслова + особа + *to*: *tell, ask, advise, warn, remind, invite, encourage, beg*.
3. Після дієслова — *-ing*: *admit, deny, suggest, recommend*.
4. Дієслово + прийменник + *-ing*: *apologise for, insist on, accuse sb of, blame sb for, congratulate sb on, thank sb for*.
5. *suggest* — ніколи не *suggest sb to do*: або *suggest -ing*, або *suggest that…*

## Пастки перекладу

- «Він запропонував мені піти» — *He suggested that I go / He suggested going*, а не *suggested me to go*.
- «Вибачився за запізнення» — *apologised for being late*.
- «Звинуватив її в крадіжці» — *accused her of stealing*.
- «Пояснив мені» — *explained to me*, а не *explained me*.

## Типові помилки

- ✗ *She suggested me to go.* → ✓ *She suggested **going** / **that I go**.*
- ✗ *He denied to take the money.* → ✓ *He denied **taking** the money.*
- ✗ *She apologised to be late.* → ✓ *She apologised **for being** late.*
- ✗ *He accused her in stealing.* → ✓ *He accused her **of** stealing.*
- ✗ *They explained me the rule.* → ✓ *They explained the rule **to me**.*
- ✗ *He objected to pay.* → ✓ *He objected to **paying**.*
- ✗ *He explained me why he was late.* → ✓ *He explained **to me** why he was late.* / *He explained why he was late.*

Про основні правила зсуву часів — «Reported speech».
