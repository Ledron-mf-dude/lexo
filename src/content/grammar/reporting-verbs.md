Замість нейтрального `said` у непрямій мові часто вживають **точніше дієслово**, яке передає намір. Схема після дієслова залежить від нього.

## Схеми

| Схема | Дієслова | Приклад |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **+ that-clause** | say, explain, admit, claim, complain, mention, suggest, insist | She **admitted that** she was wrong. |
| **+ to-infinitive** | refuse, promise, offer, agree, threaten, demand | He **refused to help**. |
| **+ особа + to-infinitive** | advise, ask, tell, warn, remind, invite, encourage, order, persuade | She **advised me to rest**. |
| **+ -ing** | admit, deny, suggest, recommend, mention | He **denied taking** the money. |
| **+ прийменник + -ing** | apologise for, insist on, accuse sb of, blame sb for, thank sb for, congratulate sb on | She **apologised for being** late. |

## Приклади: пряма → непряма

| Пряма мова | Reported |
| --- | --- |
| "I'll pay." | He **offered to pay**. / He **promised to pay**. |
| "I didn't take it." | He **denied taking** it. |
| "Don't go alone." | She **warned me not to go** alone. |
| "Why don't we go out?" | He **suggested going** out. / He **suggested that we go** out. |
| "Sorry I'm late." | She **apologised for being late**. |
| "You stole the money!" | He **accused her of stealing** the money. |
| "Well done!" | They **congratulated me on winning**. |

## Suggest: три способи

- *She suggested **going** to the cinema.*
- *She suggested **(that) we go** / **we should go** to the cinema.*
- Не **suggest me to go**: *She suggested me to go* — помилка.

## Заперечення

`not` ставимо перед інфінітивом: *He **told me not to worry**.* / *She **promised not to tell**.*

Про основні правила зсуву часів — «Reported speech».
