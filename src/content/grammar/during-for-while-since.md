## For і since

| Слово | Що далі | Приклад |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **for** | **тривалість** (скільки) | I've lived here **for** ten years. |
| **since** | **точка початку** (з якого моменту) | I've lived here **since** 2015. |

- ***for** two hours, **for** a week, **for** a long time*
- ***since** Monday, **since** 8 am, **since** I was a child*

З **Present Perfect** (`have lived`): ***How long** have you lived here?*

## From … to / until

**З … до**: *I work **from** 9 **to** 5.* / *from Monday **until** Friday.*

## During і while

| | Що далі | Приклад |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **during** | **іменник** (подія, період) | I fell asleep **during** the film. |
| **while** | **підмет + дієслово** (речення) | I fell asleep **while** I was watching a film. |

## For чи during?

| For | During |
| --- | --- |
| **скільки** триває | **коли** відбулося (в межах) |
| I slept **for** eight hours. | I woke up **during** the night. |
| We stayed **for** two weeks. | We visited many places **during** our stay. |

## Ago

**Ago** — назад від зараз, з Past Simple: *I moved here two years **ago**.* (не з Present Perfect).

## For + Present Perfect / Past Simple

| Час | Приклад | Зв'язок з теперішнім |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Present Perfect** | I've worked here **for** two years. | ще працюю |
| **Past Simple** | I worked there **for** two years. | більше не працюю |

## Швидка перевірка

1. He's been ill (for / since) Monday. → **since**
2. I slept (for / during) eight hours. → **for**
3. I met her (during / while) I was travelling. → **while**
4. She called (during / for) the meeting. → **during**

## Ago чи before?

| ago | before |
| --- | --- |
| назад **від тепер** | раніше **від іншого моменту в минулому** |
| з Past Simple | часто з Past Perfect |
| *She left two years **ago**.* (два роки тому) | *She had left two years **before**.* (за два роки до того) |

`Before` без числа — просто «раніше»: *I've seen this film **before**.*

## Since як «тому що»

`Since` на початку речення ще й означає причину: ***Since** it's late, let's go home.* Див. «Contrast, purpose, reason, result (although, so that, because of, therefore)».

## Типові помилки

- ✗ *I've lived here since ten years.* → ✓ *…**for** ten years.*
- ✗ *I fell asleep during I was watching TV.* → ✓ *…**while** I was watching TV.* / *…**during** the film.*
- ✗ *I have moved here two years ago.* → ✓ *I **moved** here two years ago.*
- ✗ *I slept during eight hours.* → ✓ *I slept **for** eight hours.*
- ✗ *She had left the company two years ago.* → ✓ *She had left the company two years **before**.*

Див. «Present Perfect», «Прийменники часу».
