## At, in, on

| Прийменник | Уявлення | Приклади |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **in** | усередині (простір, місто, країна) | **in** a room, **in** Paris, **in** Ukraine, **in** the box, **in** the water |
| **on** | на поверхні, лінії | **on** the table, **on** the wall, **on** the floor, **on** Baker Street, **on** the first floor |
| **at** | точка, місце-мета, адреса | **at** the door, **at** the bus stop, **at** school, **at** work, **at** 10 Baker Street |

## Типові поєднання

| At | In | On |
| --- | --- | --- |
| at home | in bed | on the bus / train / plane |
| at work | in hospital | on the wall |
| at school | in a car / taxi | on the ground floor |
| at the station | in the street | on the beach |
| at the airport | in a picture / book | on a farm |
| at a party / concert | in the kitchen | on TV / on the radio |

`In a car / taxi`, але `on a bus / train / plane`.

## Відносне положення

| Прийменник | Значення | Приклад |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **next to / beside / by** | поруч | The bank is **next to** the café. |
| **near** | близько | She lives **near** the park. |
| **between** | між (двома) | The shop is **between** the bank and the café. |
| **among** | серед (багатьох) | She was **among** the winners. |
| **in front of** | перед | The car is **in front of** the house. |
| **behind** | позаду | The garden is **behind** the house. |
| **opposite** | навпроти | The school is **opposite** the park. |
| **above / over** | над | A lamp is **above** the table. |
| **below / under** | під | The cat is **under** the chair. |
| **on top of** | зверху на | **on top of** the fridge |

## Above чи over? Below чи under?

- **over / under** — прямо над / під, часто торкаючись або перекриваючи: *a bridge **over** the river*, *a cat **under** the table*.
- **above / below** — вище / нижче загалом: *the picture **above** the sofa*, *20 degrees **below** zero*.

## Ще

- *in the middle **of***, *at the top **of***, *at the bottom **of***, *on the left / right*.

Див. також «Прийменники руху: to, into, through, across, along, past» і «Прийменники часу: at, in, on, by, until, before, after».

## Типові помилки

- ✗ *in the bus* → ✓ ***on** the bus* (але *in a car*)
- ✗ *at the Paris* → ✓ ***in** Paris*
- ✗ *on the picture* → ✓ ***in** the picture*
- ✗ *between many people* → ✓ ***among** many people* (between — з двома)
