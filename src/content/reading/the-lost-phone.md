title: The Lost Phone

---
Last Saturday, Viktoria went for a walk in the park with her dog, Rex. The sun was shining, and lots of people were sitting on the grass.

While Rex was playing with a ball, Viktoria was talking to her sister on the phone. After the call, she put the phone in her jacket pocket. Then Rex ran after a cat, and Viktoria ran after Rex.

When she finally caught him, she checked her pocket. The phone wasn't there! She looked everywhere: under the benches, near the trees, next to the fountain. Nothing. She felt awful. All her photos were on that phone.

She was walking home sadly when she heard a voice behind her. "Excuse me! Is this yours?" A young man was holding her phone. "I saw it on the path near the playground," he said. "Your sister was calling, so I answered. She told me what you look like."

Viktoria was so happy that she almost cried. She wanted to give him some money, but he said no. "Just do the same for someone else one day," he smiled.

Now Viktoria always zips her pocket. And Rex? He still runs after cats.

## Граматика
- past-tenses — *While* Rex *was playing* with a ball, Viktoria *was talking* to her sister. — Дві тривалі дії водночас у минулому → Past Continuous в обох частинах (*was / were* + -ing).
- past-tenses — She *was walking* home sadly *when* she *heard* a voice. — Тривала дія (фон, Past Continuous) і коротка подія, що її перервала (Past Simple). *When* стоїть перед короткою дією.
- so-such — Viktoria was *so happy that* she almost cried. — *So* + прикметник + *that* — «такий…, що…». Показує наслідок сильного почуття.
- possessive-forms — Is this *yours*? — *Yours* стоїть без іменника: «Це ваше?» З іменником — *your phone*.
