My name is Denys. I am a student, and I live in a student hostel. My room is small, but I like it.

There is a bed next to the window. Under the bed, there are two boxes with my winter clothes. There is a desk in front of the bed, and on the desk there is my laptop and a lamp. My books are on a shelf above the desk.

There isn't a wardrobe in my room. My clothes are in a small cupboard behind the door. There are some photos of my family on the wall, between the window and the shelf.

The kitchen and the bathroom are not in my room. They are at the end of the corridor, and I share them with six other students.

My room is quite dark, because the window is small. I want a big green plant and a new lamp. Then my room will be perfect!

## Граматика
- there-is-are — *There is* a bed next to the window. … *There are* two boxes… — *There is* для однини, *there are* для множини. Так вводимо те, що є в кімнаті.
- there-is-are — *There isn't* a wardrobe in my room. — Заперечення: *there isn't* (= *there is not*). Для множини — *there aren't any…*
- prepositions-place — *under* the bed, *in front of* the bed, *above* the desk, *behind* the door, *between* the window and the shelf — Прийменники місця відповідають на питання «де?». *Between* — між двома предметами.
