Next spring, two hundred shared bikes will appear on the streets of our city. The project is being paid for by the city council and a local bank, and the first twenty stations are already being built.

The system is simple. You download an app, scan the code on a bike and ride away. When you finish, you leave the bike at any station. The first thirty minutes are free; after that, you pay a small amount for every quarter of an hour.

Not everyone is happy about the idea. Some drivers say the roads are already too busy and that new bike lanes will make traffic jams even worse. Shop owners on the main street are worried that customers won't be able to park near their shops.

Supporters of the project disagree. "If more people cycle to work, there will be fewer cars, not more," says Halyna Moroz, who runs a cycling club. "Cities in other countries have done this for years. Nobody wants to go back."

The council has promised that the bike lanes will be separated from the cars, so cyclists will feel safe. Helmets are not required, but they are strongly recommended.

Will the people of our city actually use the bikes? Nobody knows yet. But if the project is successful, another three hundred bikes will be added next year, and the stations will be opened in every district.

## Граматика
- passive-voice — The project *is being paid for* by the city council… the stations *are* already *being built*. — Пасив у Present Continuous (*is / are being* + V3): дія відбувається зараз, і важливо, що робиться, а не хто.
- passive-voice — If the project is successful, another three hundred bikes *will be added*. — Пасив у майбутньому: *will be* + V3.
- conditionals — *If* more people *cycle* to work, there *will be* fewer cars. — Реальна умова: *if* + Present Simple, далі *will*. Українською після «якщо» буває майбутній час («якщо більше людей їздитимуть»), англійською — ні.
- quantifiers — there will be *fewer* cars, not more — *Fewer* — для злічуваних (*cars*), *less* — для незлічуваних (*less traffic*).
