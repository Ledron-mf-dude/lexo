Nataliia is forty-two, and she is learning to drive. "When I was young, I couldn't afford lessons," she explains. "Then I had small children, and I didn't have time. Now my kids are teenagers, and I want to be independent."

Her first lesson was a disaster. She couldn't find the right gear, the car stopped three times, and she nearly hit a rubbish bin. "My instructor, Vasyl, was very calm," she says. "He just said, 'Again.' I felt like a child, but he never laughed at me."

After ten lessons, things are much better. Nataliia can drive in the city now, and she can park — well, almost. Parking between two cars is still her biggest problem.

Before the test, she has to do twenty more lessons and pass a theory exam on the computer. She must learn all the road signs, so she practises with an app on the bus every morning. She doesn't have to buy a car yet; she can use the driving school's car for the test.

Her teenage son thinks it's funny that his mum is a student. "But he is proud of me," Nataliia smiles. "Last week he said, 'Mum, when you get your licence, can you drive me to my football training?'"

## Граматика
- can-could-be-able-to — When I was young, I *couldn't afford* lessons. … Nataliia *can drive* in the city now. — *Could / couldn't* — уміння чи можливість у минулому, *can* — зараз.
- modals-obligation — Before the test, she *has to do* twenty more lessons. … She *must learn* all the road signs. — *Have to* — правило ззовні (вимога автошколи), *must* — сильний обов'язок; у цьому контексті обидва природні.
- modals-obligation — She *doesn't have to buy* a car yet. — *Don't / doesn't have to* — «не мусить», немає потреби. Не плутайте з *mustn't* — «не можна».
- future-time-clauses — *when you get* your licence, can you drive me…? — Після *when* про майбутнє — Present Simple (*get*), не *will get*.
