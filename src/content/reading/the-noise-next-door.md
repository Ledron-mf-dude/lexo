For three weeks, the residents of a quiet apartment block in Dnipro had been puzzled by a noise coming from flat 14. Every night, at around half past eleven, a low humming started, followed by a series of sharp knocks. The strange thing was that flat 14 had been empty since the spring.

Theories multiplied in the residents' group chat. "It must be the pipes," wrote the man from flat 12. "Old buildings make all kinds of noises." But the woman who lived directly below disagreed. "Pipes can't knock in a rhythm. Someone must be living there."

Others were less rational. A teenager on the fifth floor suggested a ghost; his grandmother told him off for scaring the younger children. Someone else claimed to have seen a light under the door, though nobody could confirm it.

Eventually, the building manager agreed to open the flat. Accompanied by three curious neighbours, she unlocked the door one evening at a quarter past eleven. The flat was dark and dusty. Nothing seemed out of place.

Then, exactly on time, the humming began. It was coming from the kitchen. Behind an old fridge, they found an automatic bread maker. The previous tenant, it turned out, had programmed it to start a loaf every night — and had left it plugged in when he moved out, with nothing inside. The knocks were the empty mixing paddle hitting the sides of the pan.

"It can't have been easy for him to forget that," the building manager laughed as she pulled out the plug. The residents of flat 13 have been sleeping peacefully ever since.

## Граматика
- modals-speculation — "It *must be* the pipes." … "Pipes *can't knock* in a rhythm. Someone *must be living* there." — Здогадки: *must* — майже впевнені, що так; *can't* — майже впевнені, що ні. *Must be living* — про те, що відбувається зараз.
- past-modals — "It *can't have been* easy for him to forget that." — *Can't have* + V3 — висновок про минуле: «навряд чи було легко». Тут — іронія.
- past-perfect — the residents… *had been puzzled* by a noise… flat 14 *had been* empty since the spring — Past Perfect: стан, що тривав до моменту розповіді.
- participle-clauses — *Accompanied by* three curious neighbours, she unlocked the door… — Пасивний дієприкметниковий зворот = *She was accompanied… and she unlocked…*
- present-perfect — The residents… *have been sleeping* peacefully *ever since*. — Відтоді й досі → Present Perfect Continuous.

## Запитання
? What was strange about the noise?
- It happened during the day.
+ It came from a flat that had been empty since spring.
- Only children could hear it.
= *The strange thing was that flat 14 had been empty since the spring.*
? Why did the woman below disagree with the "pipes" theory?
- She was a plumber.
+ Pipes can't knock in a regular rhythm.
- She had seen someone in the flat.
= *Pipes can't knock in a rhythm. Someone must be living there.*
? What did the teenager's grandmother do?
- She agreed that it was a ghost.
+ She told him off for frightening the children.
- She called the building manager.
= *His grandmother told him off for scaring the younger children.*
? What caused the noise?
- A neighbour playing music.
- Water in the pipes.
+ A bread maker that had been left on with nothing inside.
= Попередній мешканець запрограмував хлібопічку на щоніч і залишив увімкненою, *with nothing inside*. Стук — це лопатка, що б'ється об порожню форму.
? Why did the manager open the door at a quarter past eleven?
+ To be there just before the noise usually started.
- Because it was the only time she was free.
- Because the neighbours were asleep then.
= Шум починався *at around half past eleven*, тож вони прийшли трохи раніше — і звук почався *exactly on time*.
