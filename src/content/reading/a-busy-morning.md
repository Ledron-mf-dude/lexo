Marta lives in a small flat in Kyiv. She works in a library in the city centre. Every morning, her alarm rings at half past six.

Marta doesn't like early mornings. She usually stays in bed for five more minutes. Then she gets up, has a shower and makes breakfast. She always has porridge with honey and a big cup of coffee.

At a quarter past seven, she leaves home. The bus stop is near her building. The bus is often full, so Marta stands and listens to music. The trip takes twenty minutes.

The library opens at eight o'clock. Marta's job is interesting. She helps people find books, and on Fridays she reads stories to children. She is never late for work, because she loves it.

## Слова
- flat — квартира (брит.; амер. apartment)
- library — бібліотека (не «книгарня» — це bookshop)
- alarm — будильник
- ring [rings] — дзвонити
- early — ранній, рано
- stay in bed — залишатися в ліжку
- get up [gets up] — вставати (з ліжка)
- have a shower [has a shower] — приймати душ
- porridge — каша (вівсяна)
- leave home — виходити з дому
- bus stop — автобусна зупинка
- full — повний, переповнений
- trip — поїздка
- be late for [late for] — запізнюватися на / до

## Граматика
- present-simple-vs-continuous — Every morning, her alarm *rings* at half past six. — Звичка, що повторюється щодня, → Present Simple. Після she / he / it дієслово отримує -s: *rings, works, gets up*.
- present-simple-vs-continuous — Marta *doesn't like* early mornings. — Заперечення в Present Simple для she: *doesn't* + дієслово без -s (*doesn't like*, не *doesn't likes*).
- time-markers — She *usually* stays in bed… She *always* has porridge… — Слова частоти (*always, usually, often, never*) стоять перед основним дієсловом, але після *is / are*: *She is never late*.

## Запитання
? What time does Marta's alarm ring?
- At six o'clock.
+ At half past six.
- At a quarter past seven.
= *Half past six* — це 6:30. О 7:15 (*a quarter past seven*) Марта вже виходить з дому, а не прокидається.
? What does Marta do after she gets up?
- She listens to music.
- She stays in bed for five minutes.
+ She has a shower and makes breakfast.
= У другому абзаці: *Then she gets up, has a shower and makes breakfast.* П'ять хвилин у ліжку — це до того, як вона встає, а музику вона слухає в автобусі.
? Why does Marta stand on the bus?
+ Because the bus is often full.
- Because the trip is short.
- Because she likes standing.
= *The bus is often full, so Marta stands* — *so* («тому») показує наслідок: автобус повний, тож вона стоїть.
? Which sentence is true?
- Marta works in a bookshop.
- Marta is often late for work.
+ Marta reads stories to children on Fridays.
= У тексті: *on Fridays she reads stories to children.* Вона працює в бібліотеці (*library*), а не в книгарні, і ніколи не запізнюється (*She is never late*).
