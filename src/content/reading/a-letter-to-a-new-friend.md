Hi Ciara,

My name is Myroslava, but my friends call me Mira. I am from Ukraine. I live in Vinnytsia, a city in the centre of the country. I am thirty years old, and I am a nurse.

I have got a husband, Roman, and a little son, Bohdan. He is four. Roman is an engineer. We have also got a dog. His name is Chip, and he loves the snow.

In my free time, I like reading and cooking. I don't like running, but I love long walks by the river. On Sundays, I visit my parents. My mum always makes varenyky. Do you know them? They are like big dumplings with potato or cherries inside.

I want to practise my English, so I am very happy to write to you. What about you? What do you do? Have you got any brothers or sisters? What do you like doing at the weekend?

Please write soon!

Best wishes,
Mira

## Слова

- centre — центр (амер. center)
- nurse — медсестра
- husband — чоловік (у шлюбі)
- son — син
- engineer — інженер
- free time — вільний час
- reading — читання
- cooking — куховарство, готування
- walk [walks] — прогулянка
- by the river — біля річки
- visit — відвідувати
- dumpling [dumplings] — вареник, галушка
- cherry [cherries] — вишня

- practise — практикувати (амер. practice)
- at the weekend — на вихідних (амер. on the weekend)
- Best wishes — З найкращими побажаннями (наприкінці листа)

## Граматика
- to-be — I *am* thirty years old, and I *am* a nurse. — Вік і професія — з *to be*. Перед професією — *a / an*: *a nurse, an engineer*.
- have-got — I *have got* a husband… *Have you got* any brothers or sisters? — У питанні *have* виходить наперед: *Have you got…?*
- gerund-after-verbs — I like *reading* and *cooking*. I don't like *running*. — Після *like, love, don't like* — дієслово з -ing, коли говоримо про захоплення.
- questions — *What do you do?* — Питання про професію: «Ким ви працюєте?» Не плутайте з *What are you doing?* — «Що ви робите зараз?»
