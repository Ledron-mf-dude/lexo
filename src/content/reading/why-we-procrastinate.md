Most people who procrastinate are not lazy. They will happily spend three hours cleaning the kitchen, reorganising their bookshelves or answering emails — anything except the one task that actually needs doing. If procrastination were simply about avoiding effort, this wouldn't make sense.

What psychologists increasingly argue is that procrastination is a problem of managing emotions, not time. We put off tasks that make us feel bad: bored, anxious, insecure or overwhelmed. Delaying the task brings instant relief, and the brain remembers that relief. The next time a similar task appears, avoiding it seems even more attractive.

This explains why the classic advice — "just make a schedule" — often fails. The person usually knows perfectly well what needs to be done and when. What they lack is a way of coping with the unpleasant feeling the task triggers.

Several strategies seem to help. One is to make the first step absurdly small. Instead of "write the report", the task becomes "open the document and write one sentence". Starting is the hardest part; once people have begun, they tend to carry on.

Another is to stop criticising yourself. It sounds counterintuitive, but studies suggest that students who forgave themselves for procrastinating before one exam procrastinated less before the next. Guilt, it seems, simply adds another bad feeling to avoid.

Finally, it helps to be honest about what you are really avoiding. Is it the task itself, or the fear that you will do it badly? Often, it is the second. Naming that fear doesn't make it disappear, but it does make it smaller.

## Граматика
- conditionals — *If* procrastination *were* simply about avoiding effort, this *wouldn't make* sense. — Друга умова: автор вважає, що це не так. *Were* — для всіх осіб у формальному стилі.
- cleft-sentences — *What psychologists increasingly argue is that* procrastination is a problem of managing emotions… — Виділення через *What … is*: спершу інтрига, потім головна думка.
- gerund-after-verbs — They will happily spend three hours *cleaning* the kitchen… *Instead of* "write the report"… — Після *spend (time)* і прийменників (*instead of, about*) — герундій.
- gerund-or-infinitive — *stop criticising* yourself — *Stop* + -ing — припинити робити щось. *Stop to criticise* означало б «зупинитися, щоб покритикувати».
- emphasis — it *does make* it smaller — Підсилювальне *do / does* у ствердженні: «але таки робить його меншим».
