title: How I Stopped Translating
level: B1

For years, I used to translate everything in my head. Someone asked me a question in English, I translated it into Ukrainian, thought of an answer, and then translated the answer back. By the time I was ready to speak, the conversation had moved on.

I knew a lot of grammar rules. I could explain the difference between the Present Perfect and the Past Simple better than some of my teachers. But I couldn't have a simple chat about the weather without feeling stressed.

Things changed when I stopped learning single words and started learning whole phrases. Instead of memorising "decision — рішення", I learned "make a decision", "a difficult decision", "I haven't made a decision yet". My brain began to store English in ready-made pieces, so I didn't have to build every sentence from zero.

The second thing that helped was listening. I started listening to podcasts while I was cooking or walking to work. At first, I understood about half. I didn't stop to look up every word; I just tried to follow the story. After a few months, I noticed that I was understanding more and more without translating.

Finally, I got used to making mistakes. I joined an online speaking club where nobody cared about perfect grammar. People cared about what I wanted to say. That made me brave.

Do I still translate sometimes? Of course. But now it feels like a dictionary I can open when I need it, not a wall between me and the language.

## Граматика
- used-to — For years, I *used to translate* everything in my head. — *Used to* + базова форма — минула звичка, якої вже немає. Заперечення й питання — *didn't use to*.
- gerund-after-verbs — I stopped *learning* single words and started *learning* whole phrases. — Після *stop* — герундій, якщо припиняємо саму дію. *Stop to look* — це інше: зупинитися, щоб подивитися: *I didn't stop to look up every word* в абзаці про подкасти.
- used-to — Finally, I *got used to making* mistakes. — *Get used to* + -ing — «звикнути до чогось». Не плутайте з *used to translate*: тут *to* — прийменник, тому далі -ing.
- past-perfect — By the time I was ready to speak, the conversation *had moved on*. — Розмова пішла далі ще до моменту, коли він був готовий, → Past Perfect для раннішої дії.
