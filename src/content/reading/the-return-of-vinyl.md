By any rational measure, the vinyl record should have disappeared decades ago. It is fragile, bulky and expensive; it has to be turned over every twenty minutes; and its sound, whatever enthusiasts may claim, is not objectively superior to a good digital file. Yet sales of records have risen steadily for more than fifteen years, and in some markets they now outsell CDs.

What is striking is who is buying them. It is not only nostalgic listeners rebuying the albums of their youth. A substantial share of buyers are in their twenties — people who grew up with music as something infinitely available and, in a sense, weightless. Surveys suggest that a fair number of them do not even own a record player, buying the records to display rather than to play.

Explanations tend to fall into two camps. The first emphasises the physical object: the large artwork, the ritual of sliding the record from its sleeve, the fact that you cannot skip a track with a thumb. Having paid for something tangible, listeners report that they pay more attention to it, often listening to an album from start to finish for the first time in years.

The second explanation is about identity. In a world where everyone has access to the same vast streaming catalogue, owning a particular record says something about who you are. A shelf of vinyl, unlike a playlist, can be seen by visitors. It is, among other things, a statement.

Both explanations point to the same underlying shift. Streaming solved the problem of access so completely that access itself stopped feeling valuable. What people seem to want now is not more music, but a different relationship with it: slower, more deliberate, and, paradoxically, more limited.

## Граматика
- past-modals — the vinyl record *should have disappeared* decades ago — *Should have* + V3 — те, що логічно мало б статися, але не сталося.
- cleft-sentences — *What is striking is who* is buying them. — Виділення через *What … is*: увага на несподіваній частині.
- participle-clauses — *Having paid* for something tangible, listeners report that they pay more attention to it… — Перфектний дієприкметник: спершу заплатили, потім уважніше слухають.
- wh-ever-words — its sound, *whatever* enthusiasts *may claim*, is not objectively superior — *Whatever* + *may* — поступка: «хоч би що стверджували».
- nominalisation — Both explanations point to *the same underlying shift*. — Процес зміни названо іменником (*shift*), що узагальнює все сказане вище.
