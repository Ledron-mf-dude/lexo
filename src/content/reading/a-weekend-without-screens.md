I had been meaning to try a "digital detox" for months, but there was always a reason to postpone it. Finally, one Friday evening, I switched off my phone, put it in a drawer together with my laptop, and asked my partner to hide the key.

The first few hours were surprisingly hard. I kept reaching for my pocket without thinking, as if my hand had a mind of its own. When I wanted to know what time the bakery closed, I realised I had no idea how to find out. In the end, I simply walked there. It was open.

By Saturday morning, something had shifted. Without the usual stream of news and notifications, the day felt longer. I read sixty pages of a novel I had started a year earlier, and I actually remembered what I'd read. I called — on the landline — a friend I hadn't spoken to properly in ages, and we talked for an hour.

Not everything was pleasant. I missed a message about a change to a work meeting, and on Sunday I got lost on the way to a friend's house because I couldn't use a map app. My partner, who had refused to join the experiment, found my frustration very entertaining.

When I switched my phone back on on Sunday night, there were 214 notifications waiting. To my surprise, only three of them actually mattered.

I haven't given up my phone, of course. But I've made a few changes. I no longer sleep with it next to my bed, I've deleted two social media apps, and on Sunday mornings it stays in the drawer. It turns out that most of the things I was afraid of missing could wait.

## Граматика
- past-perfect — I *had been meaning* to try a "digital detox" for months. — Past Perfect Continuous: намір, що тривав до того моменту в минулому.
- past-perfect — By Saturday morning, something *had shifted*. — *By* + момент у минулому → Past Perfect: зміна вже відбулася до суботнього ранку.
- unreal-past — *as if* my hand *had* a mind of its own — *As if* + минула форма — порівняння з нереальним.
- relative-clauses — My partner, *who had refused* to join the experiment, found my frustration very entertaining. — Неозначальне підрядне в комах — додаткова інформація про відому людину.
- no-longer-anymore — I *no longer* sleep with it next to my bed. — *No longer* стоїть перед основним дієсловом і звучить формальніше за *not … anymore*.
