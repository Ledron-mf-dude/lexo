My best friend is Oksana. We are both twenty-five, and we live in the same street. We know each other from school.

Oksana is short and slim, with long dark hair and brown eyes. She is very funny and a little crazy. She always has a new idea. Last week it was yoga, this week it is the guitar.

Oksana works in a bank, but she doesn't like her job. She loves animals. She has got two cats and a parrot, and she wants to be a vet one day.

We usually meet on Wednesday evenings. We cook dinner together, watch a film or just talk for hours. In summer, we often go to the river and swim.

I am quiet and Oksana is loud, but we are a good team. When I am sad, she makes me laugh. When she has a problem, I listen. That is what friends are for.

## Слова
- best friend — найкращий друг, найкраща подруга
- both — обидва, обидві
- the same — той самий, однаковий
- know each other — знати одне одного
- slim — стрункий
- funny — смішний, кумедний
- crazy — божевільний; тут — шалений, нестримний
- idea — ідея
- animal [animals] — тварина
- parrot — папуга
- vet — ветеринар
- together — разом
- talk for hours — говорити годинами
- quiet — тихий, спокійний
- make [makes] me laugh — смішити мене

## Граматика
- to-be — We *are* both twenty-five. — Вік в англійській — з дієсловом *to be*: *I am 25*, а не *I have 25*.
- present-simple-vs-continuous — Oksana *works* in a bank, but she *doesn't like* her job. — Постійні факти → Present Simple. У запереченні для she: *doesn't* + дієслово без -s.
- adjectives-adverbs — She is very *funny* and a little *crazy*. — Прикметник стоїть після *is*, не змінюючи форми. *Very* і *a little* показують ступінь.
- have-got — She *has got* two cats and a parrot. — *Has got* = «має» для she / he / it.
