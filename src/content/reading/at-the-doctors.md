Oleh has had a cold for ten days. At first, it was only a sore throat. Then he started coughing, and now he has a temperature too. This morning, his wife made him call the doctor.

"Good morning. What's the problem?" Dr Kravets asked.

"I've got a bad cough, and I feel terrible. I can't sleep at night."

"How long have you had the cough?"

"About a week."

"Have you taken any medicine?"

"Only some tea with honey and lemon."

The doctor listened to his chest and looked at his throat. "It isn't serious, but it's a chest infection. You need antibiotics. Take one tablet twice a day, after meals, for five days. Don't stop when you feel better; finish the whole packet."

"Can I go to work?"

"No, you should stay at home for at least three days. Drink plenty of water and get a lot of sleep. If you don't feel better by Friday, come back and see me."

Oleh went to the chemist's on his way home. By Thursday, his temperature was gone, and on Monday he was back at work.

## Граматика
- present-perfect — Oleh *has had* a cold *for ten days*. … *How long have you had* the cough? — Стан почався в минулому й триває досі → Present Perfect з *for* / *how long*. Українською тут теперішній час.
- present-perfect — *Have you taken* any medicine? — Питання про досвід до цього моменту, без точного часу → Present Perfect.
- imperative — *Take* one tablet twice a day… *Don't stop* when you feel better. — Інструкції лікаря — наказовий спосіб.
- conditionals — *If* you *don't feel* better by Friday, *come back* and see me. — Умова з *if* + Present Simple і наказом у головній частині.
