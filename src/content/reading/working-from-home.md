Anna has been working from home for two years now. When her company first sent everyone home, she thought it would last a few weeks. Instead, it became her new normal.

At the beginning, she loved it. She didn't have to spend an hour in traffic every morning, and she could start work in her pyjamas. She saved money on lunches and had more time for her cat. "I've never been so productive," she told her friends.

But after a few months, things started to change. Her kitchen table had become her office, and she could never really leave work. She checked her emails at ten o'clock at night and answered messages at weekends. She also missed the small talk with colleagues at the coffee machine. Some days, the only person she spoke to was the delivery driver.

So Anna made some rules for herself. She bought a proper desk and put it in the corner of the bedroom. She now starts at nine and finishes at six, and she turns off notifications after work. Twice a week, she works from a café or a coworking space, just to be around other people.

"Working from home isn't perfect," she says, "but you should treat it like a real job. If you don't set limits, nobody will set them for you."

Her company has recently offered everyone the chance to come back to the office three days a week. Anna hasn't decided yet. She thinks she will probably choose something in between.

## Граматика
- present-perfect — Anna *has been working* from home *for two years* now. — Дія почалася в минулому і триває досі → Present Perfect Continuous з *for*. Українською тут теперішній час («працює вже два роки»), англійською — ні.
- present-perfect — Anna *hasn't decided yet*. — Ще не сталося до цього моменту → Present Perfect з *yet* у кінці заперечення.
- past-perfect — Her kitchen table *had become* her office. — Те, що сталося раніше за інший момент у минулому, → Past Perfect (*had* + V3).
- modals-obligation — You *should treat* it like a real job. — *Should* — порада, а не суворий обов'язок. Після *should* — базова форма без *to*.
- conditionals — *If* you *don't set* limits, nobody *will set* them for you. — Реальна умова в майбутньому: *if* + Present Simple, у головній частині — *will*. Після *if* не ставимо *will*.

## Запитання
? What did Anna think at first about working from home?
+ That it would last only a few weeks.
- That it would be bad for her career.
- That she would never go back to the office.
= *She thought it would last a few weeks. Instead, it became her new normal.* *Instead* («натомість») показує, що вийшло інакше.
? What was NOT a problem after a few months?
- She couldn't stop thinking about work.
- She missed talking to colleagues.
+ She spent too much money on lunches.
= На обідах вона, навпаки, заощаджувала (*She saved money on lunches*). А проблеми — пізні листи (*checked her emails at ten o'clock at night*) і брак спілкування (*missed the small talk*).
? Why does she sometimes work from a café?
- Her internet at home is slow.
+ She wants to be around other people.
- Her company pays for it.
= *Twice a week, she works from a café or a coworking space, just to be around other people.*
? What does Anna mean by "If you don't set limits, nobody will set them for you"?
- Your boss will decide everything for you.
+ You must decide yourself when work starts and ends.
- Working from home has no limits.
= Ідеться про її правила: робота з дев'ятої до шостої і вимкнені сповіщення. Ніхто, крім неї самої, цих меж не встановить.
? What will Anna probably choose?
- To work only from home.
- To come back to the office every day.
+ Something in between: partly at home, partly in the office.
= *She thinks she will probably choose something in between.* Компанія пропонує три дні в офісі, але Анна ще не вирішила.
