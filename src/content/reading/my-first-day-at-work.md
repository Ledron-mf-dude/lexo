I still remember my first day at the company. It was a cold Monday in November, and I was very nervous. I woke up at six, but I didn't eat breakfast because I wasn't hungry.

I took the metro, but there was a problem on the line, and the train stopped for fifteen minutes. I arrived at the office ten minutes late. I felt terrible. My manager, Iryna, just smiled and said, "Don't worry. It happens to everyone."

She showed me my desk and introduced me to the team. There were six people, and everyone was friendly. One of them, Oleh, explained how our project worked. I didn't understand everything, but I wrote down a lot of notes.

At lunch, we went to a small café near the office. My new colleagues asked me a lot of questions about my last job and my hobbies. I told them about my old bike and my trips to the Carpathians.

In the afternoon, Iryna gave me my first task. It was a small bug in the website's login page. It took me three hours, but I fixed it! When I went home that evening, I was tired but happy. I knew I was in the right place.

## Граматика
- past-tenses — I *woke up* at six, but I *didn't eat* breakfast. — Завершені дії в минулому → Past Simple. Неправильні дієслова мають власну форму (*wake → woke, take → took*), а заперечення — завжди *didn't* + базова форма (*didn't eat*, не *didn't ate*).
- irregular-verbs — It *took* me three hours, but I *fixed* it! — *Take → took* (неправильне), *fix → fixed* (правильне, з -ed). *It took me three hours* — «у мене пішло три години».
- there-is-are — *There was* a problem on the line… *There were* six people. — У минулому *there was* для однини і *there were* для множини.
- verbs-two-objects — She *showed me* my desk… Iryna *gave me* my first task. — Після *show, give, tell* спочатку людина, потім річ: *gave me a task*. Без *to*: не *gave to me a task*.
