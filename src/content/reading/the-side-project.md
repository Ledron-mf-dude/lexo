For the last three years, Bohdan has been spending his evenings on a side project: an app that helps small bakeries manage their orders. It started as a favour for a friend who was tired of taking orders on paper. Today, forty bakeries across the country use it, and some of them pay a monthly fee.

"I wish I had known how much work it would be," Bohdan admits. "Writing the code was the easy part. Talking to users, fixing their problems at eleven at night, writing documentation — nobody tells you about that."

He has learned a great deal, though. At his day job, he works on a small part of a huge system, and he rarely sees the people who use it. With the app, he has had to do everything himself: design, databases, payments, even customer support. "My skills have grown more in these three years than in the previous five," he says. "My manager has noticed it too. I was given a lead role last spring, partly because of what I'd learned on the side."

There have been costs, too. He has missed a lot of weekends with friends, and there was a period last winter when he was so exhausted that he almost shut the project down. What saved it was a simple rule he now follows strictly: no work on the app on Fridays and Saturdays, whatever happens.

Bohdan is often asked whether he plans to quit his job and work on the app full-time. "Not yet," he says. "If it were bringing in twice as much money, I'd think about it seriously. For now, I like having a salary and a hobby that might one day become a business."

His advice to anyone thinking of starting a side project? "Solve a real problem for real people, and start charging early. If someone pays you even a little, you know they actually need what you've built."

## Граматика
- present-perfect — For the last three years, Bohdan *has been spending* his evenings on a side project. — Тривала дія з минулого дотепер → Present Perfect Continuous. Наголос на процесі, а не на результаті.
- wish-if-only — *I wish I had known* how much work it would be. — Жаль про минуле: *wish* + Past Perfect. «Шкода, що я не знав…»
- conditionals — *If* it *were bringing in* twice as much money, *I'd think* about it seriously. — Друга умова: зараз це не так. Після *if* — минула форма (*were*), у головній — *would*.
- passive-voice — I *was given* a lead role… Bohdan *is* often *asked* whether… — Пасив, коли важливий той, з ким це сталося: *I was given* — «мені дали».
