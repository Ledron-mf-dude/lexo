Good morning! Here is the weather for today.

In Lviv, it is cold and rainy. The temperature is only eight degrees. Take an umbrella and a warm jacket. You can't sit in the park today, but you can visit a museum or a cosy café.

In Kyiv, it is cloudy and windy, but it isn't raining. It is twelve degrees. It is a good day for a walk, but don't forget your hat.

In Odesa, it is sunny and warm. The temperature is twenty degrees. You can go to the beach, but the sea is still cold, so you can't swim. Wear sunglasses, and drink a lot of water.

Tomorrow it will be warmer everywhere. In the evening there will be a little snow in the mountains, so drive carefully.

Have a nice day!

## Граматика
- there-vs-it — *It is* cold and rainy. … *It is* twelve degrees. — Про погоду, температуру й час кажемо через *it*: «Холодно» = *It is cold*. Без *it* речення неповне.
- can-could-be-able-to — You *can't sit* in the park today, but you *can visit* a museum. — *Can / can't* — можливо чи неможливо. Після *can* — дієслово без *to*.
- imperative — *Take* an umbrella… *don't forget* your hat. … *drive* carefully. — Поради в прогнозі — наказовий спосіб: дієслово без підмета, заперечення з *don't*.
- adjectives-adverbs — drive *carefully* — Прислівник (з -ly) описує, як робити дію: *careful* driver, але *drive carefully*.

## Запитання
? What is the weather like in Lviv?
+ Cold and rainy.
- Cloudy and windy.
- Sunny and warm.
= *In Lviv, it is cold and rainy.* Хмарно й вітряно — у Києві, сонячно — в Одесі.
? Is it raining in Kyiv?
- Yes, a lot.
+ No, it isn't.
- Only in the evening.
= *In Kyiv, it is cloudy and windy, but it isn't raining.*
? Why can't people swim in Odesa?
- It is very windy.
- The beach is closed.
+ The sea is still cold.
= *You can go to the beach, but the sea is still cold, so you can't swim.*
? What is the weather like tomorrow?
- Colder everywhere.
+ Warmer everywhere, with a little snow in the mountains.
- Rainy in Odesa.
= *Tomorrow it will be warmer everywhere. In the evening there will be a little snow in the mountains.* *Will* — про майбутнє в прогнозі.
