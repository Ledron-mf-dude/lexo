It was almost two in the morning when Kateryna got on the night bus. She had spent the whole evening at a friend's birthday party, and she was exhausted. She sat down at the back, put her headphones on and closed her eyes for "just a minute".

When she opened them again, the bus was empty and the engine had stopped. Outside, there were no houses, only trees and a single street lamp. The driver was standing next to her. "End of the line," he said.

Kateryna's heart was racing. She had missed her stop by more than forty minutes, her phone battery had died, and she had no idea where she was. She explained the situation to the driver, almost in tears.

The driver, an older man with a grey moustache, listened patiently. "Don't worry," he said. "I have to drive back to the city centre in fifteen minutes anyway. You can stay on the bus. This time, I'll wake you up at your stop."

While they were waiting, he poured her some tea from his flask and told her about the strange things he had seen on night shifts: a man who had brought a goat on board, a wedding party in full costume, a lost parrot that kept repeating "Next stop!"

At half past three, the bus stopped outside Kateryna's building. "Your stop, sleeping beauty," the driver smiled. She has never fallen asleep on a bus since then.

## Граматика
- past-perfect — She *had spent* the whole evening at a friend's birthday party. — Що сталося раніше за момент розповіді (посадку в автобус) → Past Perfect: *had* + V3.
- past-perfect — She *had missed* her stop…, her phone battery *had died*… — Кілька подій, що вже сталися до моменту, коли вона прокинулася. Усі в Past Perfect.
- past-tenses — The driver *was standing* next to her. — Past Continuous для того, що вона побачила в момент пробудження — фонова дія.
- present-perfect — She *has never fallen* asleep on a bus *since then*. — Від тієї ночі й досі → Present Perfect з *since*.
