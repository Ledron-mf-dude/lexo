It is Saturday morning. Tom and Anya are in a small café near the park. A waitress comes to their table.

"Good morning! What would you like?"

"I'd like a cappuccino and a cheese sandwich, please," says Anya.

"And for you?"

"Can I have a black tea and an omelette?" Tom asks.

"Sorry, we haven't got any eggs today. Would you like pancakes? They're very good."

"OK, pancakes with jam, please. And some water."

"Still or sparkling?"

"Still, please."

Ten minutes later, the food is on the table. The pancakes are hot and sweet, and the sandwich is big. Anya takes a photo of her coffee. It has got a heart on it.

At the end, Tom asks for the bill. It is three hundred and twenty hryvnias. Tom pays by card and leaves a small tip. "This is a nice place," he says. "Let's come again next Saturday."

## Граматика
- would-like — "*I'd like* a cappuccino… *Would you like* pancakes?" — *Would like* — ввічливе «хотів би». *I'd* = *I would*. У питанні *would* стоїть перед підметом.
- can-could-be-able-to — "*Can I have* a black tea…?" — Ще одне ввічливе прохання в кафе. Після *can* — дієслово без *to*.
- quantifiers — We haven't got *any* eggs today. … And *some* water. — *Any* у запереченні, *some* у проханні чи ствердженні.
- imperative — "*Let's come* again next Saturday." — *Let's* + дієслово — пропозиція зробити щось разом.

## Запитання
? What does Anya order?
+ A cappuccino and a cheese sandwich.
- A black tea and an omelette.
- Pancakes with jam.
= Аня каже: *I'd like a cappuccino and a cheese sandwich, please.* Чай і омлет хотів Том.
? Why can't Tom have an omelette?
- The cook is ill.
+ There are no eggs today.
- Omelettes are only in the evening.
= *Sorry, we haven't got any eggs today.* Тому офіціантка пропонує млинці.
? What kind of water does Tom want?
- Sparkling.
+ Still.
- Hot.
= На запитання *Still or sparkling?* Том відповідає *Still, please.*
? How does Tom pay?
- In cash.
- Anya pays.
+ By card, and he leaves a tip.
= *Tom pays by card and leaves a small tip.*
