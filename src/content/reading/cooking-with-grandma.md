---
title: Cooking with Grandma
level: A2
topic: Їжа
summary: Бабуся вчить онуку готувати борщ — без рецепта, «на око».
---
My grandma has never used a recipe. "Recipes are for people who don't taste their food," she says. Last weekend, she finally agreed to teach me her borscht.

First, she put a big pot of water on the stove and added a piece of meat. "Cook it slowly for an hour," she said. "Don't hurry. Good soup needs time."

While the meat was cooking, we washed and peeled the vegetables: two beetroots, three potatoes, a carrot, an onion and half a cabbage. Grandma cut everything very fast. I was much slower, and my hands turned red from the beetroot.

"How much salt do I add?" I asked.

"Enough," she answered. I laughed, but she was serious. "Taste it. Then add a little more if you need it."

Next, she fried the onion, the carrot and the beetroot in a pan with some oil and a spoonful of tomato paste. Then everything went into the pot. At the end, she added some garlic and fresh dill.

We ate the borscht with sour cream and dark bread. It was delicious, much better than mine has ever been. I wrote everything down, but I know it won't taste the same. I think the secret ingredient is my grandma.

## Граматика
- imperative — "*Cook* it slowly for an hour… *Don't hurry*." — Інструкції й поради → наказовий спосіб: базова форма дієслова без *you*. Заперечення — *Don't* + дієслово.
- countable-uncountable — "*How much salt* do I add?" — *Salt, oil, water* — незлічувані, тому *how much*. Для злічуваних — *how many*: *How many potatoes?*
- present-perfect — My grandma *has never used* a recipe. — Досвід за все життя до сьогодні → Present Perfect з *never*. Не *never used* без *has*.
- adjectives-adverbs — Grandma cut everything very *fast*. … *Cook* it *slowly*. — Прислівник описує дію: *slowly* (з -ly), але *fast* має ту саму форму, що й прикметник.

## Запитання
? Why doesn't Grandma use recipes?
+ She thinks recipes are for people who don't taste their food.
- She can't read small letters.
- She has lost her cookbook.
= Вона каже: *Recipes are for people who don't taste their food.* Тобто треба куштувати, а не міряти.
? How long does the meat cook?
- Fifteen minutes.
+ About an hour, slowly.
- All day.
= *Cook it slowly for an hour. Don't hurry. Good soup needs time.*
? Why did the narrator's hands turn red?
- She cut her finger.
- The water was too hot.
+ Because of the beetroot.
= *My hands turned red from the beetroot.* Буряк фарбує руки, коли його чистиш і ріжеш.
? What did Grandma mean by "Enough"?
- Add one spoon of salt.
+ There is no exact amount — you taste and decide.
- Don't add any salt.
= Онука спершу сміється, але бабуся серйозна: *Taste it. Then add a little more if you need it.* Точної кількості немає — смак вирішує.
? What does the narrator think is the "secret ingredient"?
- Fresh dill.
- Tomato paste.
+ Her grandma herself.
= Останнє речення: *I think the secret ingredient is my grandma.* Тобто справа не в продуктах, а в досвіді й турботі бабусі.
