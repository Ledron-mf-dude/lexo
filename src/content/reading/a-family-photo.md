Liza is showing a photo to her friend Kate. It is from her grandmother's birthday party last month.

"This is my grandmother, in the middle. She's eighty today in the photo! She's wearing her favourite blue dress."

"And who is that man next to her?"

"That's my uncle Taras. He's my dad's brother. He's holding the cake. And these two girls are his daughters, my cousins. They're laughing at something, I don't remember what."

"Where are you?"

"Here, on the right. I'm taking a picture of the cake with my phone. And that's my mum behind me. She's talking to my aunt."

"Your family is big!"

"Yes, it is. We don't often meet, because my uncle lives in Odesa. But every year we all come to my grandmother's house for her birthday."

Kate looks at the photo again. "Everyone is smiling," she says. "It's a lovely picture."

- favourite — улюблений (амер. favorite)
- dress — сукня
- uncle — дядько
- hold [holding] — тримати
- cousin [cousins] — двоюрідний брат / сестра
- laugh [laughing] — сміятися

- lovely — чудовий, милий

## Граматика
- present-simple-vs-continuous — She's *wearing* her favourite blue dress. — Опис фото: що відбувається на знімку, → Present Continuous (*is wearing, is holding, are laughing*).
- present-simple-vs-continuous — We *don't often meet*, because my uncle *lives* in Odesa. — Що буває зазвичай і що є постійним фактом → Present Simple. Порівняйте: на фото *He's holding the cake* (зараз), але *He lives in Odesa* (завжди).
- demonstratives — *This is* my grandmother… *That's* my uncle… *These two girls*… — *This / these* — близько до мовця (на фото під пальцем), *that / those* — далі. *These* — множина від *this*.

? Whose birthday is in the photo?

+ Liza's grandmother's.
- Kate's.
= *It is from her grandmother's birthday party last month.* Бабусі на фото вісімдесят.

- Liza's father.
+ The brother of Liza's dad.
- Liza's cousin.
= *That's my uncle Taras. He's my dad's brother.* Двоюрідні сестри (*cousins*) — це його доньки.
? What is Liza doing in the photo?
- She is holding the cake.
- She is talking to her aunt.
+ She is taking a picture of the cake.
= *I'm taking a picture of the cake with my phone.* Торт тримає дядько Тарас, а з тіткою розмовляє мама Лізи.
? Why doesn't the family meet often?
+ Because Liza's uncle lives in another city.
- Because they don't like parties.
- Because the grandmother is ill.
= *We don't often meet, because my uncle lives in Odesa.* *Because* вводить причину. Усі на фото, навпаки, усміхаються.
