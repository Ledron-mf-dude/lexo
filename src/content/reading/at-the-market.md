It is Sunday, and Olena is at the market with her son, Ivan. They need some vegetables for soup and some fruit for the week.

"Good morning! How much are the tomatoes?" Olena asks.

"Forty hryvnias a kilo," the woman at the stall says. "They're very fresh. They're from my garden."

"Can I have two kilos, please? And some onions too."

"Of course. Anything else?"

"Have you got any carrots?"

"Sorry, I haven't got any carrots today. But I have got some nice potatoes."

Olena buys a bag of potatoes too. Then Ivan sees the apples at the next stall. They are big and red.

"Mum, can we buy some apples? Please!"

"Yes, we can. But only one kilo. We haven't got much money left."

At home, they make a big pot of soup. Ivan eats two apples before lunch.

## Слова
- market — ринок, базар
- vegetables — овочі
- fruit — фрукти (зазвичай без -s: some fruit)
- soup — суп
- how much — скільки (коштує)
- stall — ятка, прилавок (на ринку)
- fresh — свіжий
- garden — город, сад (біля будинку)
- onion [onions] — цибуля
- anything else — ще щось
- carrot [carrots] — морква
- potato [potatoes] — картопля
- bag — пакет, сумка
- money — гроші (незлічуване: much money)
- pot — каструля

## Граматика
- quantifiers — Have you got *any* carrots? … I have got *some* nice potatoes. — *Some* — у ствердженнях і проханнях, *any* — у питаннях і запереченнях: *I haven't got any carrots.*
- countable-uncountable — We haven't got *much money* left. — *Money* незлічуване, тому *much*, а не *many*. Так само *some fruit*, а не *some fruits*.
- can-could-be-able-to — *Can I have* two kilos, please? — *Can I have…, please?* — найпростіше ввічливе прохання в магазині. Не *I want two kilos* — це звучить різко.

## Запитання
? Why are Olena and Ivan at the market?
+ They need food for soup and fruit for the week.
- They sell vegetables there.
- Ivan wants to buy a toy.
= Перший абзац: *They need some vegetables for soup and some fruit for the week.* Продає овочі жінка за яткою, а не Олена.
? Why are the tomatoes fresh?
- They are from a shop.
+ They are from the woman's garden.
- They are very cheap.
= Продавчиня каже: *They're very fresh. They're from my garden.* Ціна (сорок гривень за кіло) — окрема інформація, вона не пояснює свіжість.
? What doesn't the woman have today?
- Onions.
- Potatoes.
+ Carrots.
= *Sorry, I haven't got any carrots today.* Цибулю й картоплю Олена купує в неї.
? Why do they buy only one kilo of apples?
- Ivan doesn't like apples.
+ They haven't got much money left.
- The apples are small.
= Олена пояснює: *But only one kilo. We haven't got much money left.* Яблука, навпаки, великі й червоні, а Іван дуже їх хоче.
