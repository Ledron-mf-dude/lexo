Boredom has had a poor press for as long as anyone has bothered to write about it. Moralists denounced it as the gateway to vice; psychologists catalogued it alongside restlessness and apathy; and the entire consumer economy might plausibly be described as an apparatus for ensuring that no one need ever experience it. We have, it seems, very nearly succeeded. Rarely is there now a moment — in a queue, on a train, in the minute before sleep — that cannot be filled.

It is worth pausing to ask what we have traded away. Boredom, at least in its milder forms, is not the absence of mental activity but a peculiar kind of it: a restless scanning for something worth attending to. Left to run its course, that scanning tends to turn inward. The mind, finding nothing in its surroundings to seize upon, begins to rearrange what it already holds. It is in this state, a number of writers and scientists have testified, that unexpected connections are made — the solution that arrives in the shower, the idea that surfaces on a long walk.

Such testimony is anecdotal, admittedly, and one should be wary of romanticising an experience that, for those trapped in monotonous work or enforced idleness, is anything but creative. There is a world of difference between the boredom one chooses and the boredom one is sentenced to. To defend the former is not to make light of the latter.

Even so, the voluntary kind deserves a better reputation than it currently enjoys. What makes it valuable is precisely what makes it uncomfortable: the refusal of the world to supply us with stimulation on demand. Only when that supply is interrupted do we discover what our own minds produce unprompted — and the discovery is not always flattering. Perhaps that, more than the minor inconvenience, is what we are really fleeing.

- inversion-c2 — *Rarely is there* now a moment… that cannot be filled. — Інверсія після заперечного прислівника на початку: *Rarely is there* замість *There is rarely*. Книжний наголос.
- inversion-c2 — *Only when* that supply is interrupted *do we discover* what our own minds produce… — Після *Only when…* інверсія переходить у головне речення: *do we discover*.
- formulaic-subjunctive — an apparatus for ensuring that no one *need* ever experience it — Subjunctive після *ensure that*: *need* без -s. Звучить піднесено-книжно; нейтрально — *needs*.

- non-finite-c2 — *To defend* the former *is not to make light of* the latter. — Інфінітив як підмет і як іменна частина: «Захищати перше не означає применшувати друге».
