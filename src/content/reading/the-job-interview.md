Olesia had prepared for the interview for a week. She had read everything about the agency, practised her answers in front of the mirror and chosen her clothes the night before. Still, when she walked into the meeting room, her heart was beating fast.

There were two people at the table: the head of marketing, Mr Dale, and a woman from human resources. Mr Dale asked her to tell them a little about herself. Olesia explained that she had worked for a small online shop for three years, where she had run their social media accounts.

Then came the question she was afraid of. "What is your biggest weakness?"

Olesia didn't say that she was "too much of a perfectionist". Instead, she told the truth. "I used to find it hard to say no," she said. "I took on too many tasks and then worked late to finish them. Now I plan my week on Monday and tell my manager early if something won't fit."

The woman from HR wrote something down. Mr Dale asked how she would promote a product with a very small budget. Olesia described a campaign she had created for the shop: they had asked customers to post photos with their orders, and sales had gone up by thirty percent.

At the end, they asked if she had any questions. She asked what a normal day in the team looked like and how they measured success.

Two days later, the phone rang. Mr Dale told her that they had chosen her. "Lots of candidates had good answers," he said, "but you were the only one who gave us real examples."

## Граматика
- past-perfect — Olesia *had prepared* for the interview for a week. — Підготовка відбулася до співбесіди → Past Perfect для раннішої дії, коли розповідь уже в минулому.
- reported-speech — Mr Dale told her *that they had chosen* her. — Непряма мова після минулого *told*: час зсувається назад (*have chosen → had chosen*).
- indirect-questions — They asked *if she had* any questions. … how she *would promote* a product. — Непряме питання має прямий порядок слів, без *do / did*: *if she had*, а не *if did she have*.
- used-to — I *used to find* it hard to say no. — Колишня звичка, якої вже немає. Олеся показує, що проблему розв'язано.
