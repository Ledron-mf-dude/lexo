title: A Rainy Holiday

summary: Тиждень на морі, коли весь час ішов дощ, — і чому родина все одно згадує його з усмішкою.
---
Two years ago, my family went on holiday to the seaside. We booked a little house near the beach for a whole week. We were dreaming of sun, swimming and ice cream.

On the first morning, I woke up early because something was knocking on the roof. It was rain. It was raining heavily, and the sky was dark grey. "It's only one day," my dad said. But it rained the next day too, and the day after that.

At first, we were angry. My little sister was crying, and my parents were checking the weather app every hour. Then my mum found a box of old board games in a cupboard. We started to play, and we couldn't stop.

While the rain was falling outside, we were sitting around the table, laughing and arguing about the rules. In the evenings, we cooked together and told funny stories. One afternoon, we put on raincoats and walked along the empty beach. The waves were huge and beautiful.

On the last day, the sun finally came out. We swam for ten minutes, but the water was freezing. Now, when someone asks about our best holiday, we all say the same thing: the rainy one.

## Граматика
- past-tenses — I woke up early because something *was knocking* on the roof. — Past Continuous описує фон чи процес у минулому, Past Simple — подію (*woke up*).
- past-tenses — *While* the rain *was falling* outside, we *were sitting* around the table. — Дві тривалі дії одночасно → обидві в Past Continuous, з *while*.
- can-could-be-able-to — We started to play, and we *couldn't stop*. — *Couldn't* — минула форма від *can't*: «не могли».
- time-markers — *Two years ago*, my family went on holiday… — *Ago* стоїть після проміжку часу і вимагає Past Simple.
