Online shopping is fast and convenient, but it's also easy to waste money. Here are five common mistakes and how to avoid them.

1. Buying because of a countdown timer. "Only 2 hours left!" Timers like this are designed to make you panic. If you need to decide in two minutes, you will probably buy something you don't need. Close the tab and come back tomorrow. In most cases, the "special price" will still be there.

2. Not checking the size chart. Sizes are different in every country and every brand. A medium jacket from one shop may be a small in another. Measure yourself and compare the numbers before you order.

3. Ignoring delivery and return costs. A cheap T-shirt isn't cheap if the delivery costs more than the shirt. Also check whether returns are free. Some shops charge you for sending items back.

4. Trusting every review. Many reviews are written by real customers, but some are paid for. Be careful if all the reviews are perfect and were posted on the same day. Look for reviews with photos and details.

5. Saving your card on every website. It's convenient, but if a small shop is hacked, your card details could be stolen. Use a payment service or a virtual card if your bank offers one.

The golden rule? If you wouldn't buy it in a real shop, don't buy it online.

## Граматика
- conditionals — *If* you *need* to decide in two minutes, you *will* probably *buy* something you don't need. — Реальна умова: *if* + Present Simple, у головній частині — *will*.
- passive-voice — Timers like this *are designed* to make you panic. … Many reviews *are written* by real customers. — Пасив у Present Simple: *am / is / are* + V3. Важливо, що робиться, а не хто.
- passive-voice — your card details *could be stolen* — Пасив після модального: *could be* + V3 — «можуть бути вкрадені».
- conditionals — *If* you *wouldn't buy* it in a real shop, *don't buy* it online. — Уявна ситуація (*wouldn't buy*) і порада-наказ у головній частині.
