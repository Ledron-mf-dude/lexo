It began with a single post in a neighbourhood chat. In the autumn of 2022, a retired teacher named Valentyna asked whether anyone had spare blankets for families who had just arrived in the district. By the evening, her hallway was full of bags.

Two years later, what started in Valentyna's flat has become a warehouse run by more than sixty volunteers. Every week, food, clothes and medicine are sorted, packed and delivered to around four hundred households. The space was offered free of charge by a local businessman, and the vans are lent by a delivery company on Sundays.

The volunteers are a remarkably mixed group. There are students who come between lectures, a programmer who built the system they use to track deliveries, and pensioners who are said to be the fastest packers in the building. "Nobody here is a professional," Valentyna says. "We learned everything by doing it — and by making mistakes."

Those mistakes taught them a lot. In the first months, donations were accepted without any list, so the warehouse was soon full of things that nobody needed, while basic items like nappies and tinned food kept running out. Now families are asked what they need before anything is collected, and the team publishes a weekly list of the items they are short of.

Keeping people motivated has been harder than finding donations. Volunteers get tired, and many have their own difficulties. The group insists that everyone takes at least one week off every two months, and the coordinators make a point of thanking each person by name.

Valentyna admits she sometimes wonders how long they can continue. "But then somebody comes in to collect a parcel and tells you their child finally has warm boots," she says, "and you remember why you started."

## Граматика
- passive-voice — Food, clothes and medicine *are sorted, packed and delivered*… — Пасив у Present Simple: регулярний процес, а виконавці очевидні або неважливі. Кілька дієприкметників ділять одне *are*.
- passive-reporting-have-something-done — pensioners who *are said to be* the fastest packers — *Be said to* + інфінітив: «кажуть, що вони…». Безособова передача чужої думки.
- subjunctive — The group *insists that* everyone *takes* at least one week off… — Після *insist that* у британській англійській звично Present Simple; формальніший варіант — *everyone take* (subjunctive).
- relative-clauses — what *started* in Valentyna's flat *has become* a warehouse — *What* = «те, що»: увесь підрядний вираз — підмет речення.
