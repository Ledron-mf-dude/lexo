Three years ago, Halyna Bondar opened a café in the centre of her town. Twelve months later, she closed it, having lost most of her savings. Today she runs a successful bakery two streets away. Asked what she learned from the first attempt, she doesn't hesitate.

"If I had done proper research, I would never have chosen that location," she says. "It looked perfect — a busy street, lots of people walking past. But most of them were on their way to work. They didn't have time to sit down for coffee. If I'd stood outside for a week and counted who actually stopped, I'd have known."

Her second mistake was trying to please everyone. The menu grew from twelve items to almost forty, because every customer suggestion seemed worth adding. "We ended up doing many things averagely and nothing brilliantly," she admits. "I wish I'd had the courage to say no."

The third mistake was financial. Halyna paid for expensive furniture and a coffee machine before she knew whether the business would work. "I should have started small. I could have rented equipment for the first six months."

When she opened the bakery, she did almost everything differently. She chose a spot near a residential area where people bought bread every morning. She offered only a handful of products, but made them exceptionally well. And she kept her costs low until the bakery had paid for itself.

"People talk about failure as if it were the opposite of success," she says. "For me, it was the most expensive course I've ever taken — and the most useful one."

## Граматика
- conditionals — *If I had done* proper research, *I would never have chosen* that location. — Третя умова: уявна ситуація в минулому, яка не сталася. *If* + Past Perfect, *would have* + V3.
- wish-if-only — *I wish I'd had* the courage to say no. — Жаль про минуле: *wish* + Past Perfect.
- past-modals — I *should have started* small. I *could have rented* equipment… — *Should have* + V3 — критика власного минулого рішення; *could have* — нереалізована можливість.
- participle-clauses — she closed it, *having lost* most of her savings. … *Asked* what she learned… — Дієприкметникові звороти: *having* + V3 — дія раніше за головну; *Asked…* = *When she is asked…*
- unreal-past — People talk about failure *as if it were* the opposite of success. — *As if* + *were* — порівняння з тим, що авторка вважає неправдою.
