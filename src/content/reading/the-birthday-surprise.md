---
title: The Birthday Surprise
level: A2
topic: Свята
summary: Друзі готували для Марка вечірку-сюрприз, але все пішло не за планом.
---
Last Friday was Mark's thirtieth birthday. His girlfriend, Yulia, and his friends decided to organise a surprise party for him.

The plan was simple. Yulia was going to take Mark to the cinema at six o'clock. Meanwhile, the friends were going to decorate the flat, bring the food and hide in the dark living room. At eight, Yulia and Mark were going to come back, and everyone was going to shout "Surprise!"

But on Friday afternoon, Mark called Yulia. "I'm really tired," he said. "Can we stay at home tonight?" Yulia panicked. She quickly sent a message to the group: "Change of plan! Come at half past five, not at six!"

The friends arrived early and worked very fast. They put balloons everywhere and hid the cake in the bathroom. At six o'clock, Mark opened the door. The living room was dark and silent. He turned on the light, and twenty people jumped up and shouted.

Mark was so shocked that he dropped his keys. Then he started to laugh. "I wasn't tired at all," he admitted. "I wanted to stay at home because I was planning a surprise dinner for Yulia!"

## Граматика
- future-in-the-past — Yulia *was going to take* Mark to the cinema. — *Was / were going to* — план у минулому, який міг і не здійснитися. Тут план змінився.
- past-tenses — Mark *opened* the door… He *turned on* the light, and twenty people *jumped up*. — Послідовність подій у розповіді → Past Simple. Зверніть увагу на правопис: *panic → panicked*, *drop → dropped*.
- so-such — Mark was *so shocked that* he dropped his keys. — *So* + прикметник + *that* — наслідок сильного почуття.
- past-tenses — I *was planning* a surprise dinner for Yulia. — Past Continuous — процес, що тривав у минулому. Марк сам щось готував, коли друзі робили свій план.
