Nazar and his friend Ostap are planning a weekend in Lviv. They are going to take the night train on Friday and come back on Sunday evening.

"The train is cheaper than the plane," says Nazar, "and it's more comfortable than the bus. We can sleep all the way there."

On Saturday morning, they are going to walk around the old town. Ostap wants to climb the tower of the City Hall. "The view from the top is the best in the city," he says. "You can see all the roofs and churches."

For lunch, they are going to try a famous café where the waiters bring your coffee in a funny way. In the afternoon, Nazar wants to visit a museum, but Ostap would prefer to sit in a park. "We'll do both," Nazar decides. "The museum is small. It won't take long."

On Saturday night, there is a jazz concert in a little bar near the main square. They have already bought the tickets.

There is only one problem: the weather. The forecast says it is going to be colder and wetter than in Kyiv. "I'm going to take my warmest jacket," says Ostap, "and an umbrella. I don't want to spend the weekend in a wet T-shirt."

## Граматика
- future-forms — They *are going to take* the night train on Friday. — План, про який уже домовилися, → *be going to*. Так само *Ostap is going to take his warmest jacket.*
- future-forms — "*We'll do* both," Nazar decides. — Рішення, ухвалене в момент мовлення, → *will*. Порівняйте: план заздалегідь — *going to*, рішення зараз — *will*.
- comparatives-superlatives — The train is *cheaper than* the plane… it's *more comfortable than* the bus. — Короткі прикметники отримують -er (*cheaper, colder*), довгі — *more* (*more comfortable*). Після порівняння — *than*.
- comparatives-superlatives — The view from the top is *the best* in the city. — Найвищий ступінь: *the* + *-est / most*. *Good → better → the best* — неправильна форма.
