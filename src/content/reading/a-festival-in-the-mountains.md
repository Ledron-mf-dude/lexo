Last weekend I went to a small music festival in the Carpathians, and it was such a good experience that I'm already planning to go back next year.

The festival takes place in a valley which is surrounded by forest, about an hour from the nearest town. There are no big stars. Instead, there are local bands, folk musicians and young singers who are just starting their careers. The music goes on until two in the morning, and in the afternoons there are workshops where you can learn traditional dances or try to play the trembita, a very long wooden horn.

The atmosphere was so friendly that I made new friends on the first evening. People share food, help each other with their tents and sing together around the fires. I didn't see a single fight all weekend.

Of course, it wasn't perfect. The weather in the mountains changes quickly, and on Saturday night there was such a heavy storm that some tents were flooded, including mine. The toilets were also a problem: there weren't nearly enough of them for two thousand people.

If you're looking for luxury, this isn't the festival for you. But if you love live music, nature and people who smile at strangers, it's the best weekend of the summer. Just bring good boots, a waterproof tent and a warm jumper — the nights are much colder than you think.

## Граматика
- so-such — it was *such a good experience that* I'm already planning to go back — *Such* + (*a*) + прикметник + іменник + *that*. Перед самим прикметником — *so*: *so friendly that*.
- relative-clauses — a valley *which is surrounded* by forest… young singers *who are* just starting their careers… workshops *where* you can learn… — *Which* — для речей, *who* — для людей, *where* — для місць.
- passive-voice — some tents *were flooded*, including mine — Пасив у Past Simple: *was / were* + V3.
- quantifiers — there *weren't nearly enough* of them for two thousand people — *Not nearly enough* — «далеко не достатньо». *Enough* стоїть перед іменником.
