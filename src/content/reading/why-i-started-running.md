I used to hate running. At school, I always came last in races, and as an adult I didn't do any sport at all. I sat at a desk all day and on the sofa all evening.

Everything changed three years ago, when my doctor told me that my blood pressure was too high. "You need to move more," she said. "Thirty minutes a day would make a big difference." I didn't want to join a gym, so I decided to try running.

My first run was embarrassing. I managed to run for two minutes before I had to stop and walk. My legs hurt for three days. But I downloaded a beginner's app that told me exactly when to run and when to walk, and I followed it three times a week.

After two months, I could run for thirty minutes without stopping. After a year, I finished my first ten-kilometre race. I didn't win, of course, but I wasn't last either.

I have been running for three years now, and my blood pressure is normal. But the biggest change isn't physical. Running has become my thinking time. Many of my best ideas for work have come to me in the park at seven in the morning.

If you're thinking about starting, my advice is simple: go slowly. Most beginners stop because they try to do too much too soon. It's better to finish every run feeling that you could do a bit more.

## Граматика
- used-to — I *used to hate* running. — *Used to* — минула звичка чи стан, якого вже немає.
- present-perfect — I *have been running* for three years now. — Дія почалася в минулому і триває → Present Perfect Continuous з *for*.
- present-perfect — Running *has become* my thinking time. — Результат змін, важливий зараз → Present Perfect.
- gerund-or-infinitive — I *decided to try* running. … I *managed to run* for two minutes. — Після *decide, manage, want* — інфінітив з *to*.
- too-enough — they try to do *too much too soon* — *Too* = «занадто»: більше, ніж треба.
